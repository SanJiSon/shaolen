Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 February 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Shaolen Goals (“Shaolen”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Shaolen Telegram mini app, the accompanying web dashboard, and our integrations with Google services.

Information we collect

Account data

  • Telegram user ID, username, and profile name (provided by Telegram when you launch the mini app).
  • Email address if you contact support or join our mailing list.

Product data

  • Missions, goals, sub-goals, habits, reminders, completion statuses, and associated notes.
  • Analytics generated inside the app (habit streaks, mission progress, etc.).

Google APIs data

  • Google Calendar: calendar IDs and event IDs that correspond to exported missions, goals, and habits. We do not read existing events from the user’s calendar; we only create/update/delete the events that belong to Shaolen Goals.
  • Google Fit: daily step counts needed for analytics widgets. We fetch only the data required for the selected time range.

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in our database so that synchronisation can happen in the background. You can revoke access any time inside Shaolen Goals or via Google Account → Security → Third-party apps.

How we use information

  • Provide the core functionality of missions, goals, habit tracking, and analytics.
  • Synchronise deadlines and reminders with Google Calendar.
  • Display Google Fit metrics inside the habit dashboard.
  • Send optional notifications or reminders via Telegram (when you explicitly enable them).
  • Monitor service health, prevent abuse, and improve the product.

Sharing and disclosure

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share Google user data with third parties except the Google APIs that the user authorizes.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or integrity of Shaolen Goals.

Data retention and deletion

  • Task-related data is stored as long as your Shaolen account exists.
  • OAuth tokens are deleted immediately when you disconnect Google inside the app or revoke access from your Google Account.
  • You may request complete deletion by messaging support@shaolen.app. We will erase all records (missions, habits, analytics, tokens) within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Access the data you have stored in Shaolen Goals at any time through the app.
  • Export, edit, or delete missions, goals, habits, and reminders.
  • Disconnect Google services and revoke OAuth access.
  • Contact us to request data reports or deletion.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices such as HTTPS, encryption at rest for OAuth tokens, regular backups, and strict access controls. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Please notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

Children’s privacy

Shaolen Goals is not directed to children under 16. If you believe we have collected data about a minor, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product or legal changes. Significant updates will be announced inside the app. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the revised policy.
