Shaolen Goals

Shaolen Goals is a productivity app that lives inside Telegram. It helps users plan missions, set goals, track daily habits, and synchronize important milestones to Google services so that the entire routine stays in sync across devices.

Why we use Google APIs

  • Google Calendar: export missions, goals, sub-goals, and habit reminders into the user’s personal calendar to keep deadlines visible.
  • Google Fit: read daily steps to motivate healthy routines inside the habit tracker.

Every Google integration is strictly opt-in. Users connect their Google account via OAuth from the in-app settings. Access tokens are stored encrypted in our database and can be revoked at any moment from either Shaolen Goals or the Google security dashboard.

Core features

  • Missions with nested sub-goals, deadlines, and progress tracking.
  • Daily habits with reminders, recurring schedules, and analytic dashboards.
  • Goal prioritization, pinning, swipe actions, and archive management.
  • Calendar synchronization to avoid missing deadlines.
  • Inline Telegram commands to insert the current to-do list directly into chats.
